What Is the All Church Retreat?

The All Church Retreat is a free, faith-centered event designed to encourage men, women, teens, and children to strengthen the family and community as a whole. Held on the 3rd Saturday of March each year, this one day retreat features engaging sessions with practical topics for everyday life. Guests will also enjoy breakfast, lunch, workshops, vendors, entertainment, gifts, and prizes.
