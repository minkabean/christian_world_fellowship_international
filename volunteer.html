Ways to volunteer

Event Planner
Plans and coordinates ministry events, including retreats, conferences, workshops, community programs, and special events. Responsible for event logistics, scheduling, registration, vendors, volunteers, communications, and day-of-event coordination.
Teens Ministry Staff
Supports the spiritual growth, development, and engagement of teenagers through Bible studies, fellowship, outreach, and engaging activities. Develops SEL activities that encourage communication, confidence, and healthy decision-making.
Children’s Ministry Staff
Supports the spiritual, educational, social, and emotional development of children. We are seeking individuals with professional experience in education, nursing, social work, juvenile services, counseling, or childcare.
Kitchen Staff
Prepares and/or serves food for ministry events while maintaining high standards of food safety, sanitation, and quality. Appropriate food-service certification or licensing required.
Christian Entertainment
Supports the planning, coordination, and presentation of Christ-centered entertainment — comedy, music, theater, dance, spoken word — identifying talented, faith-aligned performers for our events.
Social Media Promotions
Promotes ministry events and activities across social media platforms. Creates and shares engaging content, graphics, photos, videos, and promotional campaigns to increase awareness and engagement.
Moving & Setup
Assists with the transportation, setup, breakdown, and organization of equipment and materials for ministry events. Must be dependable, physically capable, and willing to work as part of a team.
Security
Provides a safe, secure, and welcoming environment for ministry events. Monitors entrances and event areas, assists with crowd awareness and emergency procedures. Security, law enforcement, or military background preferred.
Advisory Professional
Provides professional expertise and strategic insight to support the Board of Directors and organizational leadership — governance, finance, legal, business, education, technology, HR, or marketing.
Sponsorship Support
Supports the development and coordination of sponsorship opportunities. Identifies potential sponsors and builds relationships with businesses and community partners to secure financial or in-kind support.
Grant Writer
Researches and identifies grant opportunities aligned with the ministry’s mission. Develops grant proposals, narratives, budgets, and supporting documents to help secure funding.
